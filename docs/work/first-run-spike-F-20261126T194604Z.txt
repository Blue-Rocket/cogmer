Spike F: whether a page on the user's computer can use a passkey through a page under cogmer.ai

What it found: yes, in Chrome and in Safari on the Mac. A page at http://127.0.0.1:4799 sent the user
to /sign/ under cogmer.ai and got back, by navigation alone, a passkey signature over a challenge it
chose, which it verified against the public key it kept at registration. A passkey made for the site
cogmer.ai on probe.cogmer.ai signed on a.probe.cogmer.ai as well. No hosts-file entry, local
certificate or request from one page to the other was used. Both passkeys were saved to iCloud
Keychain and both signed on the user's iPhone, where the chooser showed them with nothing to tell
them apart.

Runbook: docs/work/cowork-first-run-spikes.md, section F, at 98f3521 on host-probes.
Started 2026-11-26T19:46:04Z, on the user's Mac.
